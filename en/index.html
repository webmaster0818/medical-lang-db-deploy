How to read the language support levels

◎Accepts this language
The facility reports it can see patients in this language, or has a medical interpreter or coordinator for it.
○Phone or video interpreter
Support in this language is through a phone or video interpreting service.
△Translation device
The facility has a translation device and relies on it for this language.
―Contact before visiting
The language is listed, but no interpreter or translation device is recorded. Please contact the facility before visiting.

Information is reported by each facility to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. Hours and language support can change. Please call before you visit.
