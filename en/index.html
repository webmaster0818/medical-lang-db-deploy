How to read the language support levels

◎Staff or interpreter
The facility reports it can see patients in this language.
○Phone or video interpreter
Support is through a remote interpreting service.
△Translation device
Support relies on a translation device, or the facility asks you to consult first.
―Ask before visiting
The language is listed, but the facility asks you to consult first and has no translation device.

Information is reported by each facility to the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. Hours and language support can change. Please call before you visit.
